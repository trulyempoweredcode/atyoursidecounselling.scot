Meet the people at your side

You'll work with a fully qualified, insured and BACP-registered counsellor. Get to know Patrick and Julie, their training, the issues they support and the way they work.

Counsellor & Psychotherapist

Patrick Goodlet, M.Sc.

My name is Patrick Goodlet and I am a fully trained, qualified and insured counsellor and psychotherapist. I am a qualified relationship and couples counsellor.

I provide an open, professional, welcoming and supportive environment, working in a completely non-judgemental and confidential way. I am passionate about helping clients find and release the inner person which is within everyone.

I have extensive experience in relationship counselling, bereavement counselling, work-related issues, depression, anxiety, anger management and low self-esteem.

Counsellor & Psychotherapist

Julie Kellock, Pg. Dip.

My name is Julie Kellock and I am a fully trained, qualified and insured counsellor and psychotherapist. I have a speciality in working with young adults and children, as well as adults.

I have experience working with adults, children and young people in a range of environments for various presenting issues, such as depression, anxiety, anger management, bereavement, relationships and various others.

I offer a warm, welcoming and supportive environment and will work beside you, providing a confidential, safe space to explore any difficulties you are experiencing. I am also a Certified Facilitator of the Brick-by-Brick Programme — certified LEGO® brick-based therapy.
