Counselling & Talking Therapies

Here to help you grow

Counselling provides a private, confidential space to talk through whatever you may be experiencing — a difficulty, a dissatisfaction with life, or a loss of a sense of direction or purpose. We're here to support individuals, couples, children and families across Livingston, West Lothian and Lanarkshire.

  • BACP registered & insured
  • For individuals, couples & families
  • In person, online & by telephone

Welcome

An open, warm and non-judgemental space

Our counsellors have experience of working with a full range of issues and client groups. We hold BACP membership, are fully qualified and insured, and work within the ethical guidelines set down by national governing bodies.

We offer an open, warm, non-judgemental and genuine therapeutic relationship — and an environment where you can begin a process of exploration, healing and personal growth. We have experience of working with all age groups, in both long-term and short-term work.

Why At Your Side

Therapy at the pace that suits you

Admitting to yourself that you need someone to talk to is a strength, not a weakness. Here's what you can expect from working with us.

Qualified & Registered

Both counsellors are fully qualified, insured and members of the BACP, working within recognised ethical guidelines.

Confidential

What you share stays between you and your counsellor, within the BACP ethical framework. Your privacy is respected.

Non-Judgemental

A warm, genuine and accepting relationship where you can be heard without fear of judgement.

Flexible Appointments

Appointments seven days a week — morning, afternoon and into the evening — with same-day bookings sometimes available.
